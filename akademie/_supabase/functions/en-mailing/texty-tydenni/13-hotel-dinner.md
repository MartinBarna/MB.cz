Hi {{first_name}},

The work dinner is at 10:30 pm. You're in a hotel. Someone once told you that anything you eat after 6 pm goes straight to your waist.

A bread roll at 10 pm isn't more fattening than the same roll at 8 am. Your body cares about energy in and energy out. Eat less than you burn over the week and you lose fat, whatever the clock says. There's no switch at 6 pm that stores the meal as fat.

Timing still plays a part. In a trial of women on the same weight loss plan, the group eating an earlier dinner lost more weight than the group eating late in the evening. So timing counts, just far less than the day total. Part of the gap may be fewer evening snacks, though the trial can't say for sure.

The trip usually fails the other way. You eat almost nothing all day to pay for the late dinner, then the dinner and the snacks after it push the day over. Keep a real dinner, even a late one. Put protein and fiber on that plate so you aren't raiding the minibar an hour later.

Fit the hotel dinner into the day total. That's the number that counts.
