Hi {{first_name}},

The work dinner is at 22:30. You're in a hotel. Someone once told you that nothing after 6 pm goes straight to your waist.

A bread roll at 10 pm isn't fatter than the same roll at 8 am. Your body cares about energy in and out over the day. The clock doesn't decide that. Eat less than you burn across that day and you lose weight. There's no switch at 6 pm that stores the meal as fat.

Timing still matters a little. In a trial of 82 women on the same weight loss plan, dinner at 19:00 to 19:30 led to 6.7 kg lost in 12 weeks. Dinner at 22:30 to 23:00 led to 4.8 kg lost. An earlier dinner can help a little, mostly because the evening snacks shrink. It helps a bit, that's all.

The trip usually fails the other way. You eat almost nothing all day to pay for the late dinner, then the dinner and the food after it are where the day goes over. Keep a real dinner, even a late one. Put protein and fiber on that plate so the night isn't a second meal an hour later.

Fit the hotel dinner into the day total. The day total is what decides.
