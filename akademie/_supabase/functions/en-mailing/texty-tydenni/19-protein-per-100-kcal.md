The front of the pack can say high protein. The table is the part that tells you if that's true for the calories.

Read protein and energy from the same column. Then the serving size drops out of the comparison.

Protein per 100 kcal = protein grams / kcal x 100.

If that column says 20 g of protein and 100 kcal:

20 / 100 x 100 = 20 g of protein per 100 kcal.

If it says 10 g of protein and 200 kcal:

10 / 200 x 100 = 5 g per 100 kcal.

The first line gives you 4 times as much protein for the calories. Those two lines are examples of the arithmetic, not two foods from a shop. Use the numbers printed on the pack in front of you.
