Hi {{first_name}},

Two weeks on the same plan, and the weekly average on the scale hasn't moved.

One week is too short to judge. Water, food still in the gut, and for women the phase of the cycle can move the scale by two kilos inside that week. The swing says nothing about fat.

Weigh every morning after the toilet, and compare weekly averages. If the average stays flat for two weeks in a row, and your log is accurate (weighed, oils and drinks included), change one thing:

cut about 10% of calories, or add 2,000 steps a day.

One of the two. Not both in the same week.

Skip the extra bans. Throwing out bread and cutting fruit won't help when the total intake doesn't fit.
