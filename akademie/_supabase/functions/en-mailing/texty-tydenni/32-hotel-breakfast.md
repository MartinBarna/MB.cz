The hotel breakfast is included and the buffet is open all morning. It's easy to eat it just because it's paid for, and because breakfast is "the most important meal of the day".

That line was popularized by food marketing about a century ago.

The trials: in 2014, Emily Dhurandhar and colleagues randomly told people trying to lose weight to eat breakfast or skip it. No discernible effect on weight loss. In 2019, a BMJ meta-analysis of randomized trials found that people who added breakfast tended to eat more across the day, about 260 kcal on average. Breakfast wasn't a reliable weight loss tool.

Skipping it won't slow your metabolism either. The energy you spend digesting food follows how much you eat across the day, whatever time you start.

On a trip, ask yourself one thing: does breakfast help you stay on plan, or does it get in the way?

- Eat it if you're hungry in the morning, if you train early, or if skipping it makes the evening hunger hit hard.
- Feel free to skip it if you're not hungry and you only eat it because it's there.

If you take medication for blood sugar or have a history of disordered eating, check with your doctor before changing when you eat.

One rule either way. Don't skip breakfast "for fat loss" and then make up for it at night. If that happens to you, eat the breakfast, with protein in it.
