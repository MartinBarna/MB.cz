The hotel breakfast is included and the buffet is open all morning. A lot of people eat it because it's paid for, and because breakfast is "the most important meal of the day".

That line comes from advertising.

The trials: in 2014, a group around David Allison randomly told people to eat breakfast or skip it. No clinically meaningful difference in weight. In 2019, a BMJ meta-analysis of randomized trials found that people who added breakfast tended to eat a bit more across the day. Breakfast wasn't a reliable weight loss tool.

Skipping it won't slow your metabolism either. The energy you spend digesting food follows how much you eat across the day, whatever time you start.

So on a trip the question is simple. Does breakfast help you hold the day, or does it get in the way?

- Eat it if you're hungry in the morning, if you train early, or if skipping it makes the evening hunger hit hard.
- Skip it calmly if you're not hungry and you only eat it because it's there.

One rule either way. Don't skip breakfast "for fat loss" and then make up for it at night. If that happens to you, eat the breakfast, with protein in it.
