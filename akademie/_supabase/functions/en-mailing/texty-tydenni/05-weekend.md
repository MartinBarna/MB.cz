Monday to Thursday runs on a plan. Friday night is the part nobody wrote down. By Sunday a lot of people have already decided to start again on Monday.

Weekends are a common reason fat loss stalls. People eat more on Saturday and Sunday than on weekdays, and the weekend slows weight loss down or stops it.

Two findings are easy to skip. The stricter the food rules, the more overeating shows up, and the results get worse. People who tie food to guilt lose weight worse than people who can enjoy the meal and move on. Guilt starts the next round.

Friday morning, take 5 to 10 minutes. Pick 1 or 2 meals you actually want this weekend and write them down. Then tiredness at the table has less room to add a third meal you never chose.

At the bigger meal, start with protein and vegetables. A solid portion is about 100 to 150 g of meat, fish or quark (a fresh cheese, similar to strained Greek yogurt), plus vegetables. Protein is one of the most filling parts of the meal, so the rest of the plate matters less.

A planned bigger meal has a few marks: you chose it ahead of time, protein and vegetables are on the plate, and the next morning is a normal day. An unplanned one looks different. Nobody chose it, it goes on until you feel sick, and Monday becomes the restart.

If you went over, Monday is a normal day, without starving, detox or long cardio as a penalty. You can eat 100 to 200 kcal less for two or three days.

If you overeat again and again until you feel sick, you hide it, or you lose control around food and it scares you, talk to your doctor. An email can't help you with that.
