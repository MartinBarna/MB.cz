Monday to Thursday runs on a plan. Friday night is the part nobody wrote down. By Sunday a lot of people have already decided to start again on Monday.

Weekends are a common reason fat loss stalls. Saturday and Sunday often bring more food than weekdays. That alone can slow fat loss, or stall it.

Two things are easy to skip. Very strict food rules often end in overeating. And guilt around food tends to make losing weight harder than enjoying the meal and moving on.

Friday morning, take 5 to 10 minutes. Pick 1 or 2 meals you actually want this weekend and write them down. Then when you're tired on Saturday night, there's already a plan, and a third big meal is less likely to just happen.

At the bigger meal, start with protein and vegetables. A solid portion is about 100 to 150 g of meat, fish or quark, plus vegetables. Protein is one of the most filling parts of a meal, so you're less likely to keep eating once the plate is empty.

You can tell a planned bigger meal by a few things: you chose it ahead of time, protein and vegetables are on the plate, and the next morning is a normal day. An unplanned one looks different. Nobody chose it, it goes on until you feel sick, and Monday becomes the restart.

If you went over, Monday is a normal day, without starving, detox or long cardio as a penalty. You can eat 100 to 200 kcal less for two or three days.

If you overeat again and again until you feel sick, you hide it, or you lose control around food and it scares you, talk to your doctor. That's not your fault, and it's something a doctor or specialist can really help with.
