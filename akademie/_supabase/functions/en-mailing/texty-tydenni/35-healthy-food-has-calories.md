Organic, fit, raw, superfood. Labels like that make people feel they can eat as much as they want. Your body counts the calories either way.

Some "healthy" foods are dense:

- Nuts and nut butters. A good food, and a handful is fine. Half a jar of peanut butter a day can run past a few hundred extra kcal.

It works the other way too. A sane amount of chocolate won't make you gain. No food is clean or dirty on its own. Amount and context decide.
