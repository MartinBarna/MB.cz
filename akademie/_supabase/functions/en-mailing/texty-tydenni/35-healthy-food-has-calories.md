Organic, raw, "guilt-free", superfood. Labels like that make people feel they can eat as much as they want. Your body counts the calories either way.

Some "healthy" foods are dense:

- Nuts and nut butters. A good food, and a handful is fine. Half a jar of peanut butter is close to 1,000 kcal.
- Oil, including the best olive oil. A tablespoon of oil has about as many calories as ten sugar cubes.
- Avocado. Half at breakfast is fine. Three a day will show up.
- Granola, muesli bars, "guilt-free" desserts. They often have as much sugar and fat as a regular sweet, with a nicer wrapper and a higher price.

It works the other way too. A sane amount of chocolate won't make you gain. No food is clean or dirty on its own. Amount and context decide.
