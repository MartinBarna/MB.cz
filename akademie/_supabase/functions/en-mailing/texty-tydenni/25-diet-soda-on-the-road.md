Preheader: People who swapped sugary drinks for diet ones lost more weight than people who swapped them for water.

Hi {{first_name}},

Airport lounge, long afternoon, you want something cold. Plenty of people take the regular cola because they heard the diet one is worse. It ruins your gut, it makes you crave sweets.

The studies in people say otherwise.

When diet sodas replace sugary drinks, people lose weight and their metabolic markers improve. One meta-analysis went a step further. People who switched from sugary drinks to diet drinks ended up drinking less of the sugary kind, and they lost more weight than people who switched to water.

The gut story hasn't held up in humans either. A randomized, double-blind crossover trial on aspartame found no change in the microbiome. A later review said the same for aspartame and acesulfame-K: they don't usually affect the human microbiome.

So on the road, if it's regular or diet, take the diet one. Water works too. The sugary drink is where calories leak, because you don't even notice you drank them.

One condition. This works when the rest of your day has enough protein and vegetables. A diet cola won't rescue a day of pastries. And if you have a diagnosis, ask your doctor.
