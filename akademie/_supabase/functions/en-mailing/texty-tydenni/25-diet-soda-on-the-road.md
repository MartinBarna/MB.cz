Preheader: Swapping regular cola for diet cuts the calories. The gut scare is shaky in people.

Airport lounge, long afternoon, you want something cold. You might reach for the regular cola because you've heard the diet one is worse. That it ruins your gut, that it makes you crave sweets.

The studies in people say otherwise.

When diet sodas replace sugary drinks, people lose a little weight and their metabolic markers improve. Switching to water points the same way, though the data on it are thinner. The loss comes from the sugar you stopped drinking. The sweetener itself doesn't burn fat.

In humans, the gut story is shaky too. A small randomized, double-blind crossover trial on aspartame and sucralose found minimal effect on the gut microbiome. A 2023 review found mixed results in people: some trials saw changes, but many randomized trials found no significant effect.

On the road, when the choice is regular or diet, take the diet one. Water is a good choice too. The sugary drink is where calories leak, because you don't even notice you drank them.

It works best when the rest of your day has enough protein and vegetables. A diet cola won't rescue a day of pastries. And if you have a diagnosis, ask your doctor.
