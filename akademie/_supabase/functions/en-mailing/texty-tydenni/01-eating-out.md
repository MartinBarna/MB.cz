The menu is open and the waiter is already looking at you.

Build the plate the same way as at home. About a quarter is protein: meat, fish, eggs, beans or lentils. About a quarter is a quality carb: potatoes, rice, or a wholegrain side. About half is vegetables. Fat is seasoning: a little oil or sauce. Drink water, unsweetened tea, or coffee without the sweet extras.

Read the menu in that order. Protein first. Vegetables next. One carb portion. Sauce on the side. Fat packs a lot of calories into a small volume, and a kitchen pouring by eye adds them fast.

Your total calories over the day and the week decide fat loss. Fit the meal you want into that total. Sweet drinks, juice and alcohol add calories. Liquid calories don't fill you up the way food does. Alcohol often leads to more food on top.

Sweets can stay too. Fit them into the day. A lifelong ban is hard to keep, and that's often when people drop the whole diet.
