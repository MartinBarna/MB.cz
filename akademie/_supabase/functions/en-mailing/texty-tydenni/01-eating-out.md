The menu is open and the waiter is already looking at you.

Build the plate the same way as at home. About a quarter is protein: meat, fish, eggs, beans or lentils. About a quarter is a quality carb: potatoes, rice, or a wholegrain side. About half is vegetables. Fat is seasoning: a little oil or sauce. Drink water, unsweetened tea, or coffee without the sweet extras.

Read the menu in that order. Protein first. Vegetables next. One carb portion. Sauce on the side. Fat packs a lot of calories into a small volume, and pouring by eye adds them fast.

Total energy over the day and the week is what decides fat loss. Fit the meal you want into that total. Sweet drinks, juice and alcohol add energy, and you don't feel them as fullness the way you feel food. Alcohol often leads to more food on top.

Sweets can stay too. Fit them into the day. A lifelong ban is hard to keep, and people often quit.
