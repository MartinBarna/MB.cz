Before you leave for dinner, look up the kcal in one serving of the drink you'll actually order.

A lighter beer (about 4% alcohol), 0.5 l, is about 185 kcal. A stronger one (about 5%), 0.5 l, is about 237 kcal. A 50 ml vodka shot is about 110 kcal. Dry white wine, 200 ml, is about 130 to 160 kcal, depending on the alcohol. Brands differ, so check yours. Sweet drinks add sugar on top of the alcohol.

On the night, stick to one kind of drink, and drink after you've eaten. If you want a record, take a photo of the plate and the glass. Write it down the next day from the photos. That's easier than opening calorie tables in front of friends.

Part of a hangover is dehydration. Alcohol dries you out. Drink water through the evening, and put a full bottle next to the bed.
