A sample fat loss day: about 1,550 kcal

📅 13 October 2026 · ⏱️ 5 min read

Author: Martin Barna · Published 13 October 2026

When people hear "fat loss meal plan" they picture a list of allowed foods and a pile of bans. The real version is duller and kinder. You lose fat when you take in less energy than you spend, for long enough. No miracle foods. This is one sample day around 1,500 to 1,700 kcal (calories), why the exact number is personal, and how to move the day up or down.

Short answer: The sample day is about 1,550 kcal and about 110 g of protein: oatmeal with milk, a banana and protein powder, yogurt with blueberries, chicken with rice and vegetables, an apple with almonds, and quark with wholegrain bread. It is a structure, not a personal plan. Your number comes from height, weight, sex, muscle and movement.

What one day around 1,550 kcal looks like

Do not copy this like a script. It is a shape, so you can see a normal day with a sane intake. I built it from ordinary food you can buy in a normal shop. No powder miracles, no superfoods. Roughly 1,550 kcal and over 110 g of protein, which is the part that matters for fat loss. Why that protein target is in how much protein.

  • Breakfast (about 400 kcal): oatmeal from 60 g of oats, 200 ml of semi-skimmed milk, 1 banana, 20 g of protein powder. About 30 g protein, 55 g carbs, 8 g fat.
  • Mid-morning snack (about 200 kcal): 150 g of white yogurt (3%) and a handful of blueberries. About 12 g protein, 20 g carbs, 5 g fat.
  • Lunch (about 500 kcal): 130 g of chicken breast, 200 g of cooked rice, a large portion of vegetables, a spoon of olive oil. About 40 g protein, 55 g carbs, 12 g fat.
  • Afternoon snack (about 150 kcal): an apple and 20 g of almonds. About 4 g protein, 18 g carbs, 8 g fat.
  • Dinner (about 300 kcal): 120 g of quark (a fresh cheese, similar to strained Greek yogurt, semi-skimmed), 60 g of wholegrain bread, slices of vegetables. About 25 g protein, 30 g carbs, 5 g fat.

Add those up and you land around 1,550 kcal and about 110 g of protein. If you need a bit more energy, one extra portion gets you toward 1,700 kcal. How, below.

Why 1,500 kcal is not your number

1,500 kcal is a good deficit for one person, too low for another, and still too high for a third. Your burn depends on height, weight, sex, how much muscle you have, and above all how much you actually move. Treat 1,500 kcal as a starting sketch, not a prescription.

Fat loss is a calorie deficit: you eat less than you spend. Estimate yours with the calculator. That number is an estimate. If the scale has not moved after two or three weeks, adjust intake a little.

Slow loss does not mean you have a "slow metabolism". Most of the time the real intake is higher than the person thinks: small bites, calories in drinks, portions guessed by eye. More in the slow metabolism myth.

Move the day up or down

The day is not concrete. Need more energy because you train hard or you do physical work? Add. Need less? Take some away. The simple move is to change carbs and fat, and keep protein high.

  • Up (about 1,700 to 1,800 kcal): add another 50 g of rice at lunch, a spoon of peanut butter at breakfast, or a piece of fruit in the evening.
  • Down (about 1,400 kcal): skip the oil at lunch, shrink the rice, and swap the almonds for vegetables.
  • More fullness at the same calories: protein, vegetables and fiber fill you more than the same calories from something light. See fiber and fat loss.
  • You like something sweet, or a beer? You do not have to cross it out. Count it. That is flexible dieting: no food is banned, you watch the total and the protein.

This is also how you stay off the classic yo-yo. A crash plan that deletes half your meals does not last. You go back to the old habits, and the weight comes with them. You keep a result with a plan you can live with, not one you only survive.

What usually breaks an otherwise good day

  • Guessing portions by eye. Weigh food at least at the start. The gap between 150 g and 250 g of rice is easily about 150 kcal.
  • Calories in drinks. Sugary drinks, juice and alcohol count. More in alcohol and fat loss.
  • Too little protein. Keep it high. It fills you and protects muscle in a deficit.
  • Too fast a pace. A very low intake tires you out more than it speeds the result. See how fast you should lose weight.

If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, on medication, or you have or had an eating disorder, talk to your doctor. This sample is not a personal plan.

Planning the next day the night before is half the job. When you already know tomorrow's meals, you do not grab the first thing in reach because you are hungry. That is meal planning for fat loss.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories should I eat to lose fat?

There is no universal number. It depends on height, weight, sex and movement. 1,500 kcal is right for one person and too low for another. Estimate your burn in the calculator and subtract a sane deficit. If the scale has not moved after two or three weeks, adjust a little.

Is a 1,500 kcal day right for everyone?

No. It is a structure, not a personal plan. For a bigger or more active person, 1,500 kcal can be too low. Use it as a sketch and fit the numbers to your burn. If you take medication or you are pregnant, talk to your doctor.

Do I have to eat exactly these meals?

No. The principle matters, not these exact foods. Swap in other foods with similar macros. That is flexible dieting. Hold the total intake and the protein. You do not have to eat this list.
