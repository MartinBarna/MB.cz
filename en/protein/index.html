Protein per 100 kcal: which foods give you the most?

When you eat less to lose fat, every calorie counts. Protein per 100 kcal shows how much protein your calories buy: 100 kcal of chicken breast, skinless (raw) = 18.8 g of protein, 100 kcal of rolled oats (dry) = 3.5 g.

I tell active people to aim for 1.6 to 2.2 g of protein per kg of body weight per day (0.7 to 1.0 g per lb). Below you find 32 foods ranked, and a calculator that turns the price in your own shop into the cost of 100 g of protein.

Why protein per 100 kcal matters

When you eat less to lose fat, protein helps you keep your muscle. You also have fewer calories to spend, so it matters how many calories come with your protein.

The same 120 g of protein comes with 640 kcal from chicken breast, skinless (raw) and with 3,232 kcal from peanut butter. That is the whole idea of this chart.

How much protein you need per day: read my article How much protein do you need.

How we count

Protein per 100 kcal

protein per 100 g ÷ kcal per 100 g × 100

Example: Chicken breast, skinless (raw): 22.5 ÷ 120 × 100 = 18.8 g.

The label “High protein per calorie”

A food gets the label when it has 10 g of protein or more per 100 kcal. I chose this line to keep the list easy to read. It is not a medical guideline.

Cost of 100 g of protein

price ÷ (pack size in g × protein per 100 g ÷ 100) × 100

Example with an imaginary price: a 1,000 g pack of chicken breast, skinless (raw) costs 10 in your currency. The pack has 1,000 × 22.5 ÷ 100 = 225 g of protein. 10 ÷ 225 × 100 = 4.44 per 100 g of protein. The price is made up, only the math is real.

Raw, cooked, dry, as sold

Cooking and drying change the water in food, so the numbers per 100 g change a lot. Chicken breast: 22.5 g of protein per 100 g raw, 31.0 g roasted. Per 100 kcal the two stay close: 18.8 g and 18.8 g. Pick the row that matches how you weigh your food. Canned beans are cooked: use the drained weight.

Frequently asked questions

What is protein per 100 kcal?

It is the grams of protein in a food divided by its calories, times 100. Chicken breast, skinless (raw) has 18.8 g of protein per 100 kcal. Rolled oats (dry) has 3.5 g. The higher the number, the more protein your calories buy.

Which foods have the most protein per calorie?

In this list the top five per 100 kcal are: Shrimp or prawns (raw) 23.6 g; Whey protein powder 22.2 g; Tuna, canned in water 22.0 g; Cod, Atlantic (raw) 21.7 g; Turkey breast (raw) 20.8 g. Protein powder is not a whole food.

How much protein do I need per day?

I tell active people to aim for 1.6 to 2.2 g of protein per kg of body weight per day (0.7 to 1.0 g per lb). With 80 kg that is 80 × 1.6 = 128 g to 80 × 2.2 = 176 g a day. If you have kidney disease or another health condition, talk to your doctor first.

Is protein per calorie the same for raw and cooked food?

Almost. Cooking changes the water in food, so protein per 100 g changes a lot. Chicken breast has 22.5 g raw and 31.0 g roasted. Per 100 kcal it stays close: 18.8 g raw and 18.8 g roasted. That is why this chart compares foods per 100 kcal.

How do I work out what protein costs in my shop?

Divide the price by the grams of protein in the pack, then multiply by 100. Grams of protein in the pack = pack size in g × protein per 100 g ÷ 100. The calculator on this page does it for you: pick the food, enter the pack size and the price, and it shows the cost of 100 g of protein.

Is high protein per calorie always better?

No. It is one number. Whole egg has 8.8 g of protein per 100 kcal, under the 10 g line, and it is still a good food. Use the chart to choose protein when calories are tight. It does not tell you what is healthy or what you enjoy.
