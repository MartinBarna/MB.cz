    What about alcohol?

    You can drink. Alcohol = 7 kcal per gram. A small glass of wine (150 ml) is roughly 120 to 130 kcal, a large restaurant pour is 150 to 220, a pint of beer is 200 to 250, and a cocktail is 200 to 500 (a creamy one is at the top of that range). We count it in the weekly numbers. Studies on younger adults found that people ate about 80 kcal more food on average after drinking, and did not eat less later to make up for it. In a bar with snacks, it is often more. So we plan the food around the drinks too.

    I have no time for the gym.

    Diet is about 80% of the result. For the rest: 8,000 steps a day and two short strength sessions a week, at home or in a hotel room, 20 to 30 minutes. If you have a gym and like it, we use it. If you don't, we don't.

    How fast will I lose weight?

    A realistic pace is 0.5 to 1% of body weight a week, so 0.5 to 1 kg a week if you weigh 100 kg. 10 kg takes roughly 3 to 5 months. Faster is possible on paper and usually costs you muscle, energy, and the result a few months later. The scale also jumps 1 to 2 kg with water and salt, so we watch the trend and your waist, not one morning.
