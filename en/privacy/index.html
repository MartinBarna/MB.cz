3. Who else processes the data

For the purposes above I use these providers as processors:

  • Supabase: the database and server functions that receive and store the forms and the visit statistics, including the technical request logs of these functions. The project runs in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • WEDOS: web hosting of martinbarna.cz (Czech Republic). Like every web host, it handles the technical data needed to deliver the pages to your browser, such as your IP address.
  • Google (Google Analytics): website statistics; cookies only after your consent in the cookie banner.
  • Meta Platforms (Meta Pixel): measuring and improving ads, only after you click "Accept" in the cookie banner.

Google and Meta may process data outside the EU, mainly in the USA. The transfer is based on safeguards under GDPR (standard contractual clauses, or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework).

I do not sell your data and I do not pass it to anyone else for their own marketing.

5. Emails

I send a short email series after the kit, and then about one email a week with tips and coaching offers, only if you ticked the consent box in the Fat Loss Kit form. Every email has a link to unsubscribe right away. You can also unsubscribe by writing to martin@martinbarna.cz. After that the emails stop.

6. How long I keep the data

I keep the data from the forms until you unsubscribe or withdraw your consent. If there is no activity from you for 3 years (no new sign-up, no reply, no application), I delete it. My own visit statistics contain no name, no email and no IP address.

If you become a coaching client, the data needed for the contract, payments and accounting are kept for the time the law requires.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to correct it, to have it deleted, to restrict its processing, to data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can withdraw your consent at any time; this does not affect what happened before you withdrew it. Write to martin@martinbarna.cz.

You can also file a complaint with the Czech data protection authority, Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (www.uoou.cz), or with the data protection authority in the country where you live or work.
