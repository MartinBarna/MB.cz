Cookbook: 42 fit recipes

42 fit recipes from ordinary ingredients you can buy in any shop. Plenty of protein, sensible calories, ready fast. Breakfasts, lunches, salads, snacks and something sweet. Pick what you like and build your own rotation.

How to read this: Each recipe has ingredients with weights, a short method, and macros for 1 portion: P = protein, C = carbs, F = fat (grams), plus a rough kcal figure. Adjust the weights to your own target. They are not sacred. Grams stay the amount to cook. Ounces and cups in brackets are approximate, so you can shop without a scale.

Breakfast

1Quark bowl with oats
200 g low-fat quark or Greek yogurt (about 7 oz), 40 g oats (about 1/2 cup), 100 g frozen fruit (about 3.5 oz), 10 g honey (about 2 tsp).
1) Pour a little milk over the oats. 2) Stir in the quark and honey. 3) Fruit on top.

Practical tip: You do not need to cook all 42. Pick 2-3 breakfasts, 3-4 main meals and a few snacks you like and that fit your budget. Make them your rotation. The less you think about food, the easier it is to stick with it.

How to keep your macros in check

  • Want more protein? Add a portion of quark, skyr, cottage cheese, or an extra egg.
  • Losing fat? Keep the protein and the vegetables, and cut the oil and the sides (rice, pasta, bread).
  • Gaining? Flip it: add the side and healthy fats (nuts, olive oil, avocado).
  • Drink. Water, tea, coffee without sugar. Liquid calories hide easily.
