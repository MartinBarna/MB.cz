How I read this

  • I change intake only after 2-3 weeks of stalled averages, and I check adherence first. Most often "the plan isn't working" because it isn't being followed, not because the numbers were wrong.
  • An average loss of 0.3-0.7% of body weight per week = the right pace. Faster than that, plus falling energy and adherence: I would rather add food than cut more.
  • Hunger, energy or sleep sitting at 1-2 for a long stretch = a warning. Before another cut, we fix sleep, stress and protein.
  • I start with the win. You will last far longer if the first thing you hear is credit for what you did, not only a list of fixes.
