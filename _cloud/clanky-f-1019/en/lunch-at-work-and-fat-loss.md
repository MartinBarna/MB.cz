Breakfast at home is under control, dinner too. In the middle of the day it is soup and a main course from the lunch menu, because at work there is no other way. When I go through a client's food log, lunch is usually the meal that is guessed the most and also the biggest plate of the day. Here is what I do about it.

## How many calories does lunch out have

The best data come from a large BMJ study (Roberts et al. 2018, PMID 30541752). Researchers bought 223 frequently ordered meals from 111 randomly selected restaurants in five countries and measured their energy directly in a lab, by burning samples in a calorimeter. No estimates from tables.

- 94% of meals from full service restaurants had at least 600 calories, and 72% of fast food meals.
- One full service meal plus one fast food meal a day covered 70 to 120% of a sedentary woman's daily needs, without drinks, starters or dessert.
- In Finland they also measured ten meals from workplace canteens. They averaged 880 calories against 1,166 in restaurants, about a quarter less.

A fried main with fries and a creamy sauce reaches a thousand calories easily. And that is one meal out of the four or five you eat in a day.

## Bigger plate, more eaten, even without hunger

Why not just eat until full? Because portion size itself changes how much we eat. In an American experiment (Rolls et al., Am J Clin Nutr 2002, PMID 12450884), 51 people were served macaroni and cheese for lunch once a week, each time a different portion. From the largest portion they ate 30% more energy than from the smallest, and they did not feel any fuller. It did not matter whether they served themselves, their sex or their weight.

Restaurants cook portions so nobody leaves hungry. Relying on fullness in front of a full plate is a plan that loses.

## How to order

1. **Choose soup by type.** Clear broth or vegetable soup fills you up cheaply. Creamy soups thickened with butter and cream carry the calories of a small main. If that is all they have, skip it.
2. **Pick the main by protein.** Roast meat, fish, chicken, eggs, beans. Something that keeps you full until the afternoon.
3. **Halve the side or leave it.** Half a portion of potatoes or rice is the easiest move in fat loss. Ask for vegetables instead of fries. It often works.
4. **Treat fried food and heavy sauces as a once a week thing,** chosen consciously, and adjust the rest of that day.
5. **Zero calorie drink.** Water, unsweetened tea or a diet soda. Half a litre of a sweet drink is around 200 calories nobody logs.

The biggest lever is knowing what you eat. Log lunch by components: a piece of meat, a side, a sauce. Not perfect, still far better than "lunch out, no idea".

## Canteen or packed box

The workplace canteen came out as the lightest option in the Finnish measurements, and it matches what I see: smaller portions, more vegetables, less frying. If you have one, you are ahead.

A packed box from home has another advantage: you know exactly what is in it. Do not turn it into a rule though. The clients who stick with it pack lunch three times a week and go out with colleagues twice. Dropping lunch with people because of a diet is a path that does not last. ## What studies say about eating out and weight

A systematic review of 28 studies (Bezerra et al., Nutr Rev 2012, PMID 22300594) found a link between frequent eating out and higher body weight in all prospective studies but one and in about half of the cross-sectional ones. The authors also point out that each study defined eating out differently and none tracked what people ate at home. It is an association, not proof that a restaurant lunch by itself makes you gain.

My conclusion is simple. You do not have to stop going out for lunch. You need to know what you order and have a few go-to choices you pick without thinking. In coaching we sort this out in the first weeks, because for people with desk jobs lunch is often the one meal that can be fixed fastest. ## FAQ

### Should I have the soup or skip it?
A clear broth or vegetable soup before the main fills you up with few calories, so go for it. Creamy soups with butter and cream are better as an occasional choice.

### How do I log a restaurant meal when I do not know the grams?
Break it into components and estimate each: meat, side, sauce, oil. It helps to weigh a similar portion at home a few times so you know what 150 g of meat or 200 g of potatoes looks like on a plate.

### Can I lose fat if I eat out for lunch every day?
Yes. You just have to count it honestly and adjust the rest of the day. People who eat out daily and still lose fat usually rotate a few proven meals and cut down the side.
