## X Article (EN)

**Title:** "I could do this if I lived alone." How to lose weight when nobody else at home is.

Your partner wants pizza. The kids have cookies in the drawer. Sunday lunch at grandma's comes with cake. You are losing weight and everyone else is not.

**Support shows up in the data**

In a 1999 study (Wing and Jeffery), people who joined a weight loss program alone and without support: 76% finished, 24% kept their results from month 4 to 10. People who joined with friends and got support: 95% finished, 66% kept it.

You do not need a buddy. But it is worth getting one ally at home.

**Your changes spread**

In the Look AHEAD trial (Gorin et al. 2008), untreated spouses of people in the intensive program lost 2.2 kg in a year vs 0.2 kg in the control group. Less high fat food ended up in the house. A 2018 trial in 130 couples found partners' weights moved together.

**One meal, different portions**

- Protein, side and vegetables served separately. You take more meat and veg, they eat as usual.
- Sauce, butter and cheese added on the plate, not in the pot.
- Lighter versions of family classics. Most families enjoy them just as much.
- Grandma's lunch: lighter breakfast, smaller side, one piece of cake without guilt.

**Sweets at home**

Kids can have them. Keep them in a closed cupboard. Fruit on the counter. Buy the sweets that do not tempt you.

**The talk**

One open conversation beats a hundred hints:
"I don't want you to eat differently because of me. Just don't offer when I say no."

When your partner says "just one bite", they rarely mean harm. Tell them what it means for you. Repeat it if needed.

## LinkedIn (CZ)

„Já bych to zvládla, kdybych bydlela sama."

Tuhle větu slyším v koučinku pořád. Partner chce řízek, děti mají v šuplíku sušenky a v sobotu se u babičky peče.

Co říkají studie:

📊 Lidé, kteří šli do hubnoucího programu s přáteli a dostali podporu, udrželi výsledek v 66 % případů. Ti, co šli sami a bez podpory, ve 24 %.
📊 Partneři lidí v intenzivním programu zhubli za rok v průměru 2,2 kg, i když sami žádnou dietu nedrželi. V kontrolní skupině 0,2 kg.

Hubnutí se doma přenáší. Bez přemlouvání.

Jak to řeším s klienty:
• jedno jídlo pro všechny, jen maso, příloha a zelenina zvlášť
• omáčka a tuk až na talíř, každý si přidá sám
• sladkosti pro děti do zavřené skříňky, na linku ovoce
• jeden otevřený rozhovor s partnerem místo sta narážek

Vařit dvě různé večeře vydrží málokdo déle než měsíc, jedno jídlo s různými porcemi klidně roky.
